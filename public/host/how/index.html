How Lock-In works

Every screen talks to one place over the internet: Lock-In, at your quiz's web address. The host console, the pub display and the team phones never need to share a network or find each other. There are no ports to open and no Chromecast or AirPlay to set up.

Guest wifi

Login pages and filters

If the guest wifi has a sign-in page or blocks sites, it must let through your quiz's web address and its live connection (WebSocket). Teams on mobile data are not affected.

If the internet drops

Nothing is lost

Phones on mobile data carry on. The night lives on the server, so any screen that loses the pub internet picks up the same night when it comes back.

The display

Two ways to show the quiz

A TV browser or Google TV device opens /display and is paired once with a 6-digit code, in Venue settings › Displays or on Run night. Or plug the host laptop in by HDMI and drag the display window to the second screen (extend, not mirror). That needs no pairing.

Media

Pictures and clips

Pictures can show on the phones as well as the display. Video and audio clips (planned) play only on the display, through the pub speakers.

Remote quiz

Possible, with a video call

Because everything runs over the internet, teams could play from anywhere. They still need to see the display, for example by sharing it on a video call.

Setup

Nothing to set up on the network

No ports, no casting, no devices finding each other. Each screen just needs a browser and an internet connection.
